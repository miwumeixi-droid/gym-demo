Entrances, one set of data

  1. Store staff: this page's link. Staff sign in with their name and 4-digit PIN. Owner / Manager sees everything; Front desk sees check-in, payments and sign-up (no totals, no phone list); other staff see only their own shift.
  2. Visiting members: the same link with #me at the end. Own card with QR, days left, visits, feedback box. Nothing about other members.
  3. Map (anyone): the separate gym-finder page.
  4. Share the page with staff as Contributor so they can record. Only you (and Editors) can read payments and feedback.

Beta limits: staff and members need a claude.ai account. The PIN keeps customers and the wrong staff out of screens, but a technical person with access could read the member list and the PINs. The real product moves this to the gym's own server.
