Bring new members to your gym

People in Ghana cannot find gyms because the map pin is often wrong and prices are not online. FitConnect shows your prices, facilities, opening hours and real directions, and sends people straight to your WhatsApp.

1. We visitWe check your location, take photos of the entrance and the floor, and write the directions from a landmark.
2. We set up your addressNo GhanaPostGPS address or Google Maps listing yet? We set them up with you on the spot, on our laptop – so people can find you everywhere, not only here.
3. You go liveYour page shows your GPS address, monthly fee, day pass, facilities, hours and events.
4. You get messagesPeople tap "WhatsApp the gym". Each month we tell you how many people viewed and messaged you.
